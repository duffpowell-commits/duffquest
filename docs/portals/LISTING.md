## Description
Duff is a line cook in Omaha with a partner, a baby, three cats and a rent check due every Monday. You've got
thirty days in November to keep it all from falling apart.

Every morning the fridge calendar tells you what kind of day it is. Work a shift at The Busted Spatula: run the
fryer, build sandwiches, prep the line. Run Madison's errand before 8 PM. Hit the Hy-Whee with fifteen bucks and a
list. Then head out after dark and settle some scores.

Porch pirates. A customer who sends his steak back three times. Coupon Carol. And a whole lineup of Madison's exes,
one at a time, each with their own arena, their own trash talk and a big comic-panel knockout. On day 24 a gold
envelope arrives: the Gala, where every ex who "made it" is waiting in one ballroom.

Fight with three-hit combos, dodge rolls and charged specials. Level up, gear up, and try to make rent.

- 17 bosses with their own arenas and knockout finishers
- Kitchen shifts, a grocery run, a Waffle Haus night shift and more side hustles
- Side quests, a phone with texts and a notes journal, and a handwritten fridge calendar for all 30 days
- Plays with keyboard, mouse or touch; your progress saves in the browser
- Settings for language, cartoon effects, screen shake and a Lite mode for older devices

## Controls (keyboard / touch)
- Move: WASD or arrow keys / left joystick
- Talk, use, attack (mash for a combo): E, Enter, Space or J / A button
- Dodge roll: Shift, K or L / B button
- Special (hold to charge): F or U / S button
- Swap special: Q / swap button
- Demon Time: R / D button
- Gear: I / G button
- Phone: P / phone icon
- Status (errand, rent, savings, bag): Tab / tap the top bar
- Settings and back: O or Esc
