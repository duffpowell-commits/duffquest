Thirty days. One rent check. Seventeen people who need a spatula to the face.

This is your life, and it's ending one paycheck at a time.

You are Duff. Line cook. Omaha, Nebraska. Full sleeves, a fox on your neck, and a burn on your wrist shaped like the edge of a flat-top. You smell like fryer oil. You will always smell like fryer oil.

You have a partner named Madison who deserved a better man and settled for a funnier one. A baby named Octavia with dimples that could end a cold war. Three cats who leave notes on the fridge like they pay rent.

They don't pay rent. You pay rent.

Barely.

Minute one: somebody's stealing the package

No tutorial wall. You open on the porch, throwing hands with a porch pirate. Minute two, Brittany puts money on you against Marketplace Mike. The rules show up after you've already thrown a punch.

Omaha is 3D now. Rent is the same.

The whole town is a painted diorama with sun shadows, street lamps that come on at dusk and a camera that leans as you walk. Vans brake for you. Neighbors step around you. Pigeons scatter when you run at them. Leaves most days, rain some evenings, snow by the end of the month.

Old phone sweating? It drops to Lite on its own. Miss the flat look? Settings > 3D view.

Brawl the block

Porch pirates. A man who sends his steak back three times. Coupon Carol, who has never paid full price for anything, including you. And Madison's exes. All of them. One at a time. Apparently she has a type, and the type is punchable.

  • 17 bosses, 17 knockouts. Burst intro cards, their own arenas, their own trash talk, their own signature moves.
  • Hits that land. Bodies squash, swings leave a smear, rolls kick up dust. Three-hit combos, dodge rolls, a charged special and Demon Time.
  • The finisher. The world slows down. Comic panels. An x-ray of the exact bone.

Work the line

Fryer, sandwich builds, salads, prep, a lineup quiz. Oil held at 350-375. Tickets that get stolen mid-build. Nineteen mini-games, each with a how-to card and an easier first go. S-rank a station and your hands remember it.

Your whole life fits on a cracked phone

The HUD is Duff's phone. Battery is your energy. Signal bars are Madison's mood. The green pill is your money, and it turns red when rent bites. Errands land as notifications.

  • Four side quests with a Notes journal and real choices. Cover for a coworker or rat her out.
  • The fridge calendar. Thirty handwritten days. Sticky notes from Madison, the baby and the cats. Thanksgiving at the Waffle Haus.
  • Side hustles. A $15 grocery run. The pharmacy. Plasma for gas money, which is a sentence you will say out loud and mean.

Day 24: The Gala

A gold envelope slides under the door. Every ex who ever "made it," in one ballroom. The influencer. The tip-app CEO. The landlord. The guy who bought your restaurant this morning.

You own one tie. It's brown.

Miss rent, pay for it

Three short Mondays and you're evicted. Get knocked out twice in a day and it's game over. Neither wipes your save: you roll back to Monday or this morning and try again.

Turn it up

Duff's own soundtrack by default. Flip Settings > EDM soundtrack and every place gets a synthesized track: future bass on the street, electro house in the kitchen, big-room drops in boss fights, dubstep at Club Cloud 9, R&B future bass on date night. Knockouts, paid rent and level-ups get their own drops.

Plays on your phone

  • Android / desktop: tap Run game, then fullscreen. Landscape.
  • iPhone, no Safari bars: open duffpowell-commits.github.io/duffquest in Safari, Share > Add to Home Screen, launch from the icon.
  • Big, spaced-out buttons for real thumbs. Tap anywhere to move dialogue on.
  • Title loads in about 10 seconds on 4G. Coming back after an update only downloads what changed.
  • Saves in your browser. Keyboard works too.

Content warning

Cartoon violence and cartoon blood (spit, flying teeth, x-ray hits; turn off with Cartoon gore). Swearing (turn on Clean language). Crude jokes about rent, exes, landlords and plasma. No sexual content. Screen shake and Lite graphics toggles in Settings.

How it was made

One line cook. One phone. No computer.

  • Code: Godot 4.5.
  • Art: from Duff's own photos and references, finished in code.
  • Music: by Duff. The EDM soundtrack, the Club Cloud 9 dubstep and the sound effects are synthesized in code, no samples. The original date theme is Brahms' Lullaby (public domain).
  • Writing: Duff's real life and jokes.
  • Fonts: Baloo 2, Nunito, Caveat, Patrick Hand (SIL Open Font License).

Some of the art and code is made with AI tools, then picked, edited and play-tested by me.
