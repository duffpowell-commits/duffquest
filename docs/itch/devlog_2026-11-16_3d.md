# Devlog: Omaha Has a Third Dimension Now (Rent Unchanged)

**Title:** Omaha has a third dimension now. Rent is the same.

**Cover image:** `docs/renders/view3d_signs.png` (or `docs/renders/view3d_canva_ground.png`)

---

Duff Quest is 3D now. On by default. I didn't ask the town, I just did it.

Nothing about your life got easier. The rent is still due Monday. The line at the Spatula still runs on grease and spite.
The porch pirates still want Madison's package more than you want to live. But now all of it has *depth*. Emotional depth,
no. Physical depth, yes. Measurable in meters.

**What's new**
- **Shadows.** Everyone casts one now, including the raccoon, who is thrilled. You can finally see the sun move across Omaha
  in real time, which is more than most of us manage on a Tuesday.
- **The camera tilts.** You're no longer a pigeon staring straight down at your own bald spot. You're a slightly taller pigeon
  at a jaunty angle.
- **The street got repainted.** The old asphalt was blurry. The new asphalt is crisp, cracked and full of manhole covers,
  exactly like the real thing, minus the smell.
- **Street lamps come on at dusk.** They didn't before. The city finally paid the bill.
- **Shop signs stay on the shops.** In the first test build the CLOSED signs floated around like ghosts who'd lost their
  jobs. They have been nailed down. Spiritually and literally.
- **Fights, home, the gala and every arena** got the 3D treatment too. Getting punched by your ex now happens in
  three axes.

**Phones**
Runs on the same phones as before. If yours starts sweating, the game quietly switches to Lite: no shadows, fewer pixels,
same rent. The download is about 0.8 MB heavier, which is roughly one honey bun of data.

**Miss the flat look?**
Settings > 3D view turns it off. Your saves don't care which way you play. Neither does your landlord.

*Heads-up: some of the art and code in Duff Quest is made with AI tools, then picked, edited and play-tested by me.*

Thanks for playing. Tell me what breaks. Something will.
