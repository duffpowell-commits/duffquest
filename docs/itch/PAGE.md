## Description

This is your life, and it's ending one paycheck at a time.

You are Duff. Line cook. Omaha, Nebraska. Full sleeves, a fox on your neck, and a burn on your wrist shaped like the edge of a flat-top. You smell like fryer oil. You will always smell like fryer oil.

You have a partner named Madison who deserved a better man and settled for a funnier one. A baby named Octavia with dimples that could end a cold war. Three cats who leave notes on the fridge like they pay rent.

They don't pay rent. You pay rent.

Barely.

Day one starts at 7 AM with a stranger in a hoodie jogging straight at Madison's package. You bonk him. That's the tutorial.

Every morning after that, the fridge calendar tells you what kind of day it is. Thirty days of November. Thirty chances to get it right. You'll get maybe twelve.

You can work the line. You can run Madison's errand before 8 PM. You can sell your plasma for gas money, which is a sentence you will say out loud and mean.

Or you can go settle something.

Porch pirates. A man who sends his steak back three times. Coupon Carol, who has never paid full price for anything, including you. And Madison's exes. All of them. One at a time. Apparently she has a type, and the type is punchable.

On day 24 a gold envelope slides under the door. The Gala. Every ex who ever "made it," in one ballroom. The influencer. The tip-app CEO. The landlord. The guy who bought your restaurant this morning.

You own one tie. It's brown.

When you win, the world slows down. Comic panels. An x-ray of the exact bone. Somebody's teeth go somewhere they can't come back from.

The body keeps the receipts.

Nobody tips the fry cook.

Hell of a girl, though.

## What you get

- **A real life, played as an RPG.** Rent on Monday. Payday Friday. A partner's mood bar that notices when you skip the couch. A college fund that laughs at you.
- **Brawl the block.** Three-hit combos, dodge rolls, a charged special, and Demon Time. Spatula first. Questions never.
- **18 bosses, 18 knockouts.** Burst intro cards, their own arenas, their own trash talk, and a comic-panel finisher with an x-ray of the bone you just broke.
- **The Gala.** Day 24. Four of Madison's exes and the man hosting them. Black tie. You brought the brown one.
- **Work the line.** Fryer, sandwich builds, prep, a lineup quiz. S-rank a station and your hands remember it.
- **The fridge calendar.** Thirty handwritten days. Sticky notes from Madison, the baby, and the cats. Thanksgiving at the Waffle Haus.
- **Side hustles.** A $15 grocery run. The pharmacy. Plasma. A Waffle Haus night shift.
- **Side quests.** Who keeps stealing the packages. A grease trap at the back door after 10 PM. A coworker's side gig you can cover for or rat out. A lost-and-found flyer. Tracked in the Notes app on Duff's phone.
- **Plays on your phone.** Landscape, touch controls, saves in your browser. Keyboard works too.
- **Toggles for the squeamish:** clean language, cartoon gore, screen shake, and Lite graphics for older phones.

## How it was made

One line cook. One phone. No computer.

- **Code:** written with Claude (Anthropic), in Godot 4.5.
- **Art:** AI-generated with Grok, Gemini, ChatGPT and Canva AI from Duff's own photos and references, then fixed and finished in code. Logo built in code from the game's fonts.
- **Music:** by Duff. The date-night theme is Brahms' Lullaby (public domain). **Sound effects:** synthesized by code.
- **Writing:** Duff's real life and jokes, drafted and edited with AI help.
- **Fonts:** Baloo 2, Nunito, Caveat, Patrick Hand (SIL Open Font License).
