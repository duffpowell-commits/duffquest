# Duff Quest privacy policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Duff Quest is a single-player game. It does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts.** You never sign in or give a name, email or phone number.
- **No ads, analytics or tracking.** The game has no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking code.
- **Your save stays on your device.** On the web, progress and settings are saved in your browser's own storage.
  On Android, they are saved in the app's private storage. Clearing browser data, or uninstalling the app, deletes
  them. They are never uploaded.
- **Network use.** The web version downloads its own game files from the site that hosts it (itch.io or GitHub
  Pages). It sends nothing about you or your play. Those hosts may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses)
  under their own privacy policies: [itch.io](https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy) and
  [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Permissions.** The Android app asks for no special permissions (no location, contacts, camera, microphone or
  storage access).
- **Children.** The game is made for adults (cartoon violence, swearing). It does not knowingly collect anything from
  anyone, children included.

If this ever changes (for example, online leaderboards), this page will be updated before that version ships.

Questions: contact the developer through the game's itch.io page, https://dufftkyosht.itch.io/duff-quest.
