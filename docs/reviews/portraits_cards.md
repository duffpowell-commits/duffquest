## Duff check (`portraits_hd/duff.png`) in detail
Compared against `docs/ref/duff/photo_front.jpg` and `photo_left_shoulder_mandala_full.jpg`.
- **D5 FAIL, wrong side.** All 3 panels turn his head toward the viewer's right, so the ear we see is his RIGHT
  ear. The fox sits under that ear, which puts it on his RIGHT neck. The photo shows it on his LEFT neck
  (viewer's right in the front photo, on the same side as the blackout arm).
- **Fox design is wrong.** It's a brown cartoon fox with hot-pink shards. The doc and photo show a realistic
  dark blue-grey fox head with **pale blue** crystal shards and a pale blue rose.
- **D2/D3: no sleeves anywhere.** Crop is bust-only, and his shoulders are in a black jacket. Strictly, D7 lets
  clothes cover the arms. But Duff's standing ask is that his sleeves show whenever he's drawn, and this is the
  portrait every other Duff image is matched against (D6), so it has to show them.
- **Outfit is off-model.** He wears a collared black jacket. His usual outfit is a black tee plus apron.
- **Passes:** the face reads as Duff (dirty-blond undercut, tied-back ponytail, blue eyes, ginger-blond stubble,
  stretched plugs plus small spiked hoops). The build can't be judged at this crop.
- **Why REDO, not FIX:** it needs a new crop that shows the arms, the fox moved and redrawn, and a clothes swap.
  That's more than one targeted edit.

## Global finding (all 21 portraits): green-screen fringe
Every portrait's semi-transparent edge pixels carry chroma-key green spill: about 1,000 to 6,000 green-tinted
edge pixels per file (worst: duff, allergy, baron, ghoster). It shows as a faint green halo on dark dialogue
backgrounds. It's uniform, so it's a script job, not an image-edit prompt: despill the alpha edges (for edge
pixels, clamp G to max(R,B)) in one batch, then re-run `godot --headless --path . --import` (R1).
