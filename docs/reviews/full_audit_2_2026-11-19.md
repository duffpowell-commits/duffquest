# Full game audit, round 2 — Nov 19, 2026

Round 1 (same day, `full_audit_2026-11-19.md`) covered save/load, side-quest logic and text. Round 2 sent four read-only
reviewers at what it didn't: combat + economy balance, the older life-sim systems, every minigame, and the web build /
performance / CI. Fixes are on branch `ccr-cac0fd1d-ozjlwu`.

## Fixed

**Minigames**
| What the player hit | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Grocery cart froze inside shelf 4** after clipping coupons or "Keep shopping" at checkout; only way out was Leave (errand failed). | Respawn points moved into the lane, plus `_free_near()` so a respawn can never land in a shelf. |
| Grocery **Leave** sits flush under the floor: a near-miss steer abandoned the errand. | Two taps within 3 s ("Sure?"). |
| Wordle **give up** 3 units above O/P: a near-miss ended the Daily and the streak. | Two taps. |
| Symphony: Clap / snack buttons appear under the NOD / RUN thumb; a late tap clapped between movements or bought a snack. | Taps ignored for 0.35 s after the screen changes. |
| Salad had **no UNDO**: one stray allergen tap capped the plate at 0.2. | UNDO button, like the sandwich. |
| Garnish: all three cherries on one rosette scored 100%. | One cherry per rosette. |
| Friday rush clock ran while the how-to card was open. | Paused while it's open. |
| Public builds loaded the family-only show & tell file (real show/plush names). | Loaded in the family edition only. |

**Combat**
| What the player hit | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Gala rematch = unlimited money farm** (+$250, +10 scrap, ~600 XP each, no clock cost; a week's rent is $195). | One rematch a day (`gala_day`, saved). |
| **Demon Time never ended** while you kept hitting (hit refunds outpaced the drain; permanent x1.5 damage). | 12-second cap. |
| **Skillet stun-locked every boss** (~95% of the fight stunned, ~20x fewer boss attacks). | Bosses get 1.6 s of stun immunity after each weapon stun. |
| Melee / Holler / Counter **erased Willnot's floor glass**, the fight's main mechanic. | Glass is skipped by all three. |
| Bass / wave / trap stuns landed during i-frames (stun right after a hit). | Not while invulnerable. |
| Golden Spatula paid $1 per target even when the hit did nothing (intros, flexes, the unkillable driver). | Only on real damage. |

**Life systems**
| What the player hit | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Quitting during the final boss's finale or the credits lost the ending for good** (no "Happily Ever After", no minivan, gala waits for day 24). | Continue plays the ending if Willnot is beaten and it hasn't been seen. |
| {partner} got a slow/migraine/good morning on a day she was already upset about rent or a missed errand (breaks the mornings rule). | The morning is dropped if she's upset after rent/errands. |

**Web / performance / CI**
| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Dialogue wrapping re-measured the line once per character, 3x a frame (~12 ms/frame on desktop, worse on phones). Dialogue is the most common screen. | Cached per line (same output, test checks it). |
| 3D labels rebuilt their text mesh every frame (NOW HIRING sign resized twice a frame; Lost & Found glints changed size). | Text/size only written when they change; glints fade instead of resizing. |
| **CI never ran test_daily**: leak words, art bake, `_pub` checks, button gaps, saves were unenforced; a failing push still shipped. | New CI step; the ship stops unless it prints ALL PASS. |
| Smoke step ignored Godot's exit code (pipe through tee). | `pipefail`. |
| Town walkers were bare shadows before world.pck landed (~23 s cold on 4G). | No walkers until it lands. |

## Left for Duff (balance / design calls)

- **Pay ladder** makes rent irrelevant by week 3 (x8.0 at the top: a full S shift is $928; rent base stays $195/week). Suggest flattening to about x1.0/1.3/1.6/2.0/2.5/3.0.
- **Energy barely matters**: a shift meal (+40 on B or better) is more than the shift costs, and every fight starts at 100 Energy and keeps it on a win ("I go in at full either way" is in the story menu, so it looks deliberate).
- **No eviction teeth**: paying at least the week's base each Monday means debt grows $15/week forever with no eviction.
- **Short XP curve**: an active player hits Lv 20 by day 14.
- **Both editions' art ships in every web pack** (family originals extractable from the public build; ~180 KB extra in street.pck). Needs separate pack builds per edition.
- `#dq=` dev links still work in release web builds (round 1).
- Smaller: Too Loud takes over day 9's grocery errand trip; catering gig accepted after midnight can push past 2 AM; rush rail covers the "?" help button; Akarui's listen phase is short.
