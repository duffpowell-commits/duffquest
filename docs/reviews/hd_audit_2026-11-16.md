# HD audit (Nov 16, 2026)

Duff's ask: "do an HD audit of the game and get everything to the sharpest resolution." He had said three
times that the sprites don't match the backgrounds' resolution.

## How the picture is made

- World coordinates: 640x360 (`project.godot`), camera zoom 2 (`main.UI_SCALE`), so one screen shows 320x180
  world units. The HUD/menus use the same 2x scale.
- Art density `s` in `scripts/art_manifest.gd` = texture pixels per world unit.
  - `s: 4` = 4 px per unit = exactly 1280x720 for a full screen.
  - `s: 2` = 2 px per unit = 640x360 for a full screen.
- Window: 1280x720, `canvas_items` stretch, integer scale (fractional on portals that waste >10%).

## Root cause (fixed in this PR)

`scripts/game.gd` rendered the whole world into a **640x360** SubViewport and blew it up with nearest
filtering. That is 2 px per world unit. Every `s: 4` asset (all 60 character sheets, Duff's outfits, 21
backdrops, 34 street props, 13 storefronts, the cats) was point-sampled down to half its pixels before it
reached the screen: every other pixel row and column thrown away. That is the jaggy, "low-res sprite on a
nice background" look.

**Fix:** the SubViewport now renders at **1280x720** (`size_2d_override` = 640x360 with stretch on), so the
game's coordinates, cameras, input, layout and hitboxes are unchanged, but each world unit gets 4 real
pixels. Every `s: 4` asset now shows 1:1. Text drawn in the world is also twice as sharp.

Side fixes that come with it:
- `shaders/hd2d.gdshader`: the bloom/far-blur offset is now two *base* pixels, so the look doesn't shrink.
- `main._veil_snapshot()`: one extra 640x360 halving step so the menu blur doesn't alias.
- `scripts/perf_guard.gd`: phones that are still under 40 fps *after* lite graphics drop the world back to
  640x360 (saved in `user://perf.cfg` as `res`). Turning lite off by hand restores 1280x720.

## Target density

**`s: 4` (4 texture px per world unit = 1280x720 per screen).** Everything new gets painted at this.
Going higher (s: 6/8) buys nothing until the render goes past 1280x720, and 4x the pixels again would hurt
phones.

## Not art problems

- Text over painted art and minigame shapes drawn with `prop()`/`draw_*` scale with the render, so they
  got sharper for free.
- Final upscale is still nearest + integer where the window allows it. On a 1920x1080 window the 1280x720
  frame goes up 1.5x; that is the one place a soft (linear) final scale could look nicer. Not changed here.
